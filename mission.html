Our Mission & Vision

Our Vision


To create a community where electronic waste is no longer a hazard but a resource, ensuring a toxic-free environment for future generations.

Our Mission


To educate the public on proper e-waste disposal, organize accessible collection drives, and partner with certified recyclers to close the loop.
